How much does a rooftop solar plant cost?

It depends on the size of the plant and on your roof. Use the savings calculator to see a suggested size, then book a free site survey. After the survey we give you an exact quote, with the subsidy taken into account where it applies.

How long until it pays for itself?

That depends on your bill, how much power you use during the day and the subsidy you get. After the survey we work it out for your house and show you the numbers before you decide.

Does it work on rainy and cloudy days?

Yes, but it makes less power. Panels run on daylight, not heat, so a cloudy day still produces some power. Sunny days make up most of the year's output. With net metering, extra units from sunny days are counted against your bill.

What maintenance does it need?

Very little. Rinse the panels with plain water now and then to wash off dust. For anything more, our service team is one call away.

Can I get a loan for it?

We guide you through loan options and help with the paperwork, so you can spread the cost instead of paying it all at once.

How much roof space do I need?

It depends on the plant size. What matters is roof area with no shade during the day. At the survey we measure your roof and tell you how many kW fit.
